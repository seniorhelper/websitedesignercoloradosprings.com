7 web design mistakes costing Colorado Springs businesses real money

These are the most common, most expensive mistakes we see when we audit local sites, and exactly how we fix each one.

Paying for looks, not leads

A site can win design awards and still produce zero revenue. Beauty without a conversion plan is an expensive decoration.

Fix: Every element we build is tied to a goal: a call, a form, a booking.

Launching on a slow, bloated template

Heavy themes and page builders pile on code visitors never see. Google's data shows the chance of a visitor bouncing rises 32% as a page's load time goes from one second to three.

Fix: We custom code lean pages that target 90+ on Google Lighthouse mobile.

Launching with zero SEO

No keyword research, no titles, no schema, no internal links. Then owners wonder why nobody finds them.

Fix: Search structure is built into every page from line one, not bolted on later.

Ignoring the phone experience

Most local searches happen on phones, and Google indexes the mobile version of your site first. A clunky mobile layout is a rankings problem and a sales problem.

Fix: We design for thumbs first, then scale up to desktop.

Ignoring AI search

ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews now answer questions that used to send clicks to websites. Vague pages do not get cited.

Fix: Clear answers, schema and entity signals make your business easy for AI to understand and recommend.

Burying the call to action

Visitors decide in seconds whether to stay. If your phone number and offer are buried, they leave for the next result.

Fix: Your number and next step sit where nobody can miss them, on every screen size.

Flying blind with no tracking

If you cannot see where calls and leads come from, you cannot improve anything.

Fix: Analytics and Search Console are connected at launch, so decisions come from data.

Bounce data: Google research as cited in Deloitte, Milliseconds Make Millions.
