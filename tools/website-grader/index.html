What a website grade really measures

Most "website graders" only look at code. Code matters, but plenty of technically perfect sites never produce a lead because the phone number is hidden, there is no price anywhere, or nothing happens after 5 pm. That is why this grader weighs the business basics too.

If your grade is lower than you would like, start with the checklist items. They are often faster to fix than speed problems, and they move the needle on calls right away. For a deeper look at the conversion side, sister brand Search Converts offers a full conversion rate optimization audit.

Website grader questions

How does the website grader calculate my grade?

It averages Google Lighthouse scores for performance, accessibility, best practices and SEO, weights that at 60 percent, and adds your business checklist at 40 percent. 90+ is an A, 80s a B, 70s a C, 60s a D, and below 60 an F.

Can I grade my site without entering a URL?

Yes. Without a URL, the grade is based on the business checklist alone.
