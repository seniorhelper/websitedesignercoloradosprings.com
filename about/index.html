Built by an operator, not just a designer.

Behind this site is Zach Wennstedt and the team at Eye To Ad Media: people who have run sales floors, owned businesses, and built websites and search programs since 2012.

Direct answer: Website Designer Colorado Springs is a web design brand of Eye To Ad Media, founded in 2012 by Zach Wennstedt and headquartered in Denver. The team builds custom-coded, SEO and AI-search ready websites for businesses in Colorado Springs, southern Colorado and around the world.

Colorado roots, real-world sales experience

Zach has deep Colorado roots. He went to kindergarten in Colorado Springs, grew up in Boulder, and spent five years in Fort Collins attending Colorado State University before leaving to build a business. He started down the marketing and SEO path in 2011 and founded Eye To Ad Media in 2012.

Before and alongside the agency, Zach was a sales manager for multi-million-dollar plumbing companies and managed national remodeling companies and their sales teams. He still runs his own businesses today. That matters for you, because every tactic we recommend gets tested on real companies with real payroll before it goes anywhere near yours.

He has also been burned by agencies and made his own marketing mistakes over the years. Those lessons are baked into how we work: no fake promises, no vanity metrics, and no guarantees on rankings, because nobody controls Google.

What we promise instead

We put your business first and treat it like our own. We give you the technology and marketing skill we use on our own companies. And we genuinely try to grow your business, every month. Learn more about the team behind Eye To Ad Media, or read more from Zach Wennstedt himself.
