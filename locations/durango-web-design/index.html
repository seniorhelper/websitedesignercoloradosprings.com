A town of about 19,000 that hosts a lot more

Durango's year-round population was about 19,000 at the 2020 Census, but the visitor economy makes it feel much bigger. Between the Durango and Silverton Narrow Gauge Railroad, the Animas River, Purgatory and the San Juan backcountry, a huge share of your customers find you on a phone, often while standing on a sidewalk deciding where to go next.

That changes what a website needs to do. It has to load fast on shaky mountain signal, show hours and booking in one tap, and make it obvious why you beat the place down the block.
